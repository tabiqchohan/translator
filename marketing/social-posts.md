## 💡 Pehli app ke liye 5 golden rules

1. **Har post me ek hi CTA** — "Download from Play Store". Zyada links = zyada confusion.
2. **Video > image > text.** Reels/TikTok/Shorts me organic reach sabse zyada hoti hai, especially niche me (Roman Urdu / Pakistan).
3. **Reply har comment ko** (24 ghante me). Algorithm + trust dono badhte hain.
4. **Screenshot-based proof** sabse zyada convert karta hai — "ye dekho maine real me" dikha do.
5. **Rating/Review mangana mat** forzar me — Play Store rules allow nahi karte incentivized reviews. 3+ translations ke baad app khud rating prompt dikhata hai (ye built-in hai ✅).
